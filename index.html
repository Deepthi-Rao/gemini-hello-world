Set Scout

An AI agent for spotting film and TV shoots around New York City, then grabbing coffee nearby and getting there.

What it can do

  • find_film_shoots

    Recent permitted shoots by borough, street, or ZIP code, from NYC Open Data.

  • find_coffee_near

    Cafes closest to an address, landmark, or NYC intersection, with walking minutes.

  • get_route

    Walk, bike, or drive directions, and which is fastest.

Try asking

Set Scout remembers the conversation until you start a new chat. Every tool it runs appears as a card above its answer; click one to see the exact arguments and result. The city's permit data can lag by a few months. Routes don't include subway or bus, and drive times ignore traffic.
